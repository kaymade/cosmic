# Changelog

Notable changes to Cosmic are documented here.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-07

### Added

- Styling for colored highlights and grouped Bases/card views
- Editor caret, active-line, and raw frontmatter treatments
- Dedicated styling for footnotes, audio and video, find and replace, notices, and hotkeys
- More complete outline, backlinks, outgoing links, and bookmarks styling
- Right-to-left layout support for directional accents and gradients
- Offline embedded versions of all six Cosmic font families and their license notices

### Improved

- Separated workspace atmospheres into colorful Nebula, dark star-field Deep Space, and plain Flat treatments
- Made Soft, Solid, and Outlined callout styles visually distinct
- Increased faint-text contrast across every light palette and Comet dark mode
- Increased code-comment contrast in the light Blue Blob, Aurora, Eclipse, Stardust, and Comet palettes
- Increased title and search-match contrast in the Original light palette
- Restored distinct semantic checkbox symbols and strengthened their contrast across light and dark palettes
- Updated the README for the live Community Themes listing
- Updated current Bases card selectors while retaining compatibility with the earlier selector
